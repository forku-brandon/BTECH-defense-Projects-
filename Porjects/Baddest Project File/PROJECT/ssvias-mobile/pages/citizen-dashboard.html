🛡️ Safety Tips for Citizens
🚗 Vehicle Safety:
• Always lock your vehicle
• Park in well-lit areas
• Install a tracking device
• Never leave keys in the car
⚠️ If You Spot a Stolen Vehicle:
• Do NOT approach the suspects
• Keep a safe distance
• Note the location and direction
