About the Bamenda Water & Health Dashboard

Empowering communities with real-time water quality information for better health outcomes

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, real-time water quality information to the Mankon community, enabling informed decisions about water safety and fostering collaboration between residents, health workers, and water authorities.

Our Core Values

🤝

Community First

We believe that communities should have access to information that affects their health and well-being.

🔬

Data Transparency

All water quality data is presented honestly, clearly distinguishing between official tests and community reports.

🌍

Sustainability

Built with open-source technology that can be maintained and expanded by the community.

📱

Accessibility

Designed for mobile devices and slow internet connections to reach everyone in the community.

💧 The Challenge We're Addressing

Before Our Platform

  • ❌ Water quality data trapped in paper records and PDF reports
  • ❌ No way for residents to know if their local water source is safe
  • ❌ No channel for community members to report water issues
  • ❌ Disconnect between water quality and health outcomes
  • ❌ Delayed response to contamination events

After Our Platform

  • ✅ Real-time water quality data on an interactive map
  • ✅ Color-coded safety status for every water source
  • ✅ Community reporting system for water issues
  • ✅ Integration with health data to show connections
  • ✅ Faster response to water quality problems

📅 Project Timeline

January 2024

Project Initiation

Needs assessment and community consultations in Mankon neighborhood. Identification of 6 key water sources.

February 2024

Data Collection & Mapping

GPS coordinates collected for all water sources. Historical test data digitized from MINEE records.

March 2024

Platform Development

Dashboard built and tested with community feedback. Mobile-optimized interface launched.

April 2024

Community Launch

Public launch with training for health workers and water officers. Ongoing community engagement.

2025+

Expansion Phase

Expanding to other neighborhoods in Bamenda. Integration with national water quality database.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the water quality data?

Official test results come from MINEE and partner NGOs. Community reports are moderated before publication. We clearly distinguish between official data and community observations.

Can I submit a report anonymously?

Yes! You can submit reports without providing your name. Your report will still help the community.

How often is the data updated?

Official test results are updated as they become available. Community reports are reviewed within 24-48 hours.

Is this available for other neighborhoods?

Currently focused on Mankon as a pilot. We plan to expand to other neighborhoods in Bamenda based on success and funding.

How can I get involved?

Register for an account to submit reports, share the dashboard with neighbors, or contact us about partnership opportunities.
